Create a world-class accessibility platform called "Silent Bridge" designed to eliminate communication barriers between deaf, hard-of-hearing, and hearing people. This product should be designed at the quality level of Apple Design Award winners, Stripe, Linear, Notion, and other premium modern software products. The design must be stunning, emotionally impactful, highly polished, responsive, and visually memorable. The product should feel innovative, purposeful, and worthy of winning a global design competition.

MISSION

Silent Bridge exists to help deaf and hearing people communicate effortlessly through camera-based gesture recognition, accessible communication tools, learning systems, custom sign creation, and emergency assistance. The product should demonstrate how technology can improve lives and make communication more inclusive.

8. Teach Silent Bridge

This is a core innovation feature.

Allow users to teach the application new signs.

Workflow:

- Open camera
- Press Record Sign
- Record gesture for several seconds
- Capture MediaPipe hand landmark data
- Preview recording
- Enter sign name
- Enter phrase meaning
- Select category
- Save sign

Example:

Gesture:
Custom family gesture

Meaning:
"I Need My Brother"

After saving:

Whenever the user performs the same gesture again:

- Detect gesture
- Match saved pattern
- Display phrase
- Speak phrase aloud

Support:

- Multiple recordings per sign
- Accuracy improvements
- Confidence scoring
- Gesture management
- Edit sign
- Delete sign

Store all custom signs in Supabase.
